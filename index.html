Welcome to Spirit

We help seniors live happier, healthier, fuller lives, with the right balance of independence, dignity, and safety, right where they feel most themselves: at home.

Our Registered Nurses, therapists, social workers, and home health aides work as one team to assess and support each patient's therapeutic, rehabilitative, and functional needs. Care is built around the individual, never the other way around.

Getting started is simple

Three steps to care at home

From your first call to your first visit, we handle the details so your family does not have to.

1

Call or refer

Reach us at 248-996-9202, or have a physician or hospital fax a referral to 248-996-9460. We answer your questions and verify Medicare coverage.

2

In-home assessment

A Registered Nurse visits to understand the medical, functional, and personal needs of your loved one, and the home they live in.

3

Personalized care plan

We build a plan around the individual and assign the right team. Care begins, and we adjust as needs change.
