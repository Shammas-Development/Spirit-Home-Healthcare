Our story

Honored to provide quality home health care while nourishing the unique spirit of every client.

Spirit Home Health Care was founded by Neil Bahura and his nephew Eymen Allos, out of personal experience. Neil spent over 12 years caring for aging parents living with dementia and cancer — managing their bathing, feeding, transportation, and medical appointments. That journey led him to start a home care agency and, together with Eymen, to create Spirit Home Health Care.

We deliver care with dignity, comfort, and patient-centered goals, and we respect each client's autonomy and right to make their own decisions. Our Registered Nurses, Physical, Occupational, and Speech Therapists, Social Workers, and Home Health Aides work together to support healing, independence, and overall well-being in the comfort of home — with culturally sensitive care in over 100 languages.
