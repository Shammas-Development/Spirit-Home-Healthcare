SPIRIT HOME HEALTH CARE — FORM SETUP (one step)
================================================

The "Request a callback" form on contact.html is fully built and will email
every submission to your intake inbox. It just needs one free access key.

HOW TO ACTIVATE (2 minutes, free, no credit card):
1. Go to https://web3forms.com
2. Enter the delivery email:  Intake@SpiritHomeHealthCare.com
3. Web3Forms emails you an "Access Key" (a long code).
4. Open contact.html and find this line:
       <input type="hidden" name="access_key" value="YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY">
   Replace  YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY  with the key they sent you.
5. Save. Done — submissions now arrive at Intake@SpiritHomeHealthCare.com.

NOTES
- Works on any hosting (no server needed).
- Spam protection (honeypot) is already built in.
- The form shows a "Sent ✓" confirmation to the visitor on success.
- The language translator (Select Language, top bar) activates automatically
  once the site is hosted on a real https:// address.
- Optional: in your Web3Forms dashboard you can turn on an auto-reply to the
  visitor and add reCAPTCHA if you ever want stronger spam filtering.
